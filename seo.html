FAQ

Local SEO, answered

What is local SEO and why does it matter?

Local SEO is the work of getting your business to show up when people nearby search for what you offer, especially in Google's map pack. It matters because map-pack listings get the majority of clicks on local searches, well ahead of the regular results below them.

How long does it take to rank higher in the map pack?

Most businesses see initial movement within four to eight weeks of profile and citation work, with rankings continuing to climb over the following months as signals compound.

What's included in Google Business Profile optimization?

Category selection, service descriptions, photos, posts, and keeping the profile active and fully filled out, all aimed at what Google and customers use to decide who ranks and who gets picked.

Do you offer local SEO without review removal?

Yes. Local SEO, Google review removal, and content takedowns are separate services, you can start with any one of them or combine them.

Does local SEO help if I already rank well in some areas?

Yes. Local rankings are hyper-specific to search intent and location, so even a business that ranks well for its main service term is often invisible for dozens of related nearby searches.

Will this help outside of Google too?

Local SEO work naturally improves visibility on Bing and Apple Maps as well, since much of it (citations, structured data, profile accuracy) feeds multiple platforms at once, though Google remains the primary focus.
