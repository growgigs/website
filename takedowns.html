FAQ

Content takedowns, answered

What is a DMCA takedown and when does it apply?

A DMCA takedown is a formal request to remove content that infringes your copyright, such as your photos, videos, or written content used elsewhere without permission. It applies whenever someone hosts your original copyrighted work without your authorization.

Can you remove a fake or impersonating social media account?

Yes. We report and pursue removal of fake profiles, impersonation accounts, and harassing posts made about you or your business across major social platforms.

What counts as defamation online?

Defamatory content is a false statement of fact, not opinion, published about you or your business that damages your reputation. We help identify eligible content and pursue its removal or correction.

How do you handle cyberbullying content?

We report targeted harassment and abusive content directly to the platforms hosting it and follow up until it's taken down.

Do you handle copyright and trademark infringement differently?

Yes. Copyright infringement (DMCA) covers original creative work like photos and text, while trademark and IP infringement covers your brand name, logo, and branded materials. Each has its own removal process, and we handle both.

How much does a takedown cost?

Cost depends on the platform, the type of content, and how many pieces are involved. Like our review removal work, you're only billed for content that actually comes down.

Can you remove content from a website that isn't a major platform?

Often, yes. DMCA takedowns apply to any host, not just major platforms. We work directly with the hosting provider or web host when there's no in-platform reporting tool available.
