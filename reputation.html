FAQ

Google review removal, answered

What makes a Google review eligible for removal?

Reviews that violate Google's content policies are eligible, including fake or spam reviews, reviews from someone who was never a customer, off-topic or unrelated content, hate speech, and reviews posted by a conflict of interest such as a competitor.

Can a real customer's negative review be removed?

Yes, often. Review platforms allow removal for more reasons than most businesses realize, and a review can qualify even if it looks genuine on the surface. We dig into the account history and how the review was posted to find the grounds for removal case by case.

How long does Google review removal take?

Most eligible reviews are resolved within days of us filing the removal request, though timing can vary depending on Google's review queue.

Do you also remove reviews from Trustpilot, Glassdoor, and Yelp?

Yes. Google is our specialty, but we also handle Trustpilot, Glassdoor, Yelp, and other major review platforms on request.

What does Google review removal cost?

There's no retainer or upfront fee. You're billed only for the reviews we actually get removed, so cost scales with results, not effort.

Is review removal guaranteed?

No legitimate service can guarantee removal, and you should be skeptical of anyone who promises it upfront. What we guarantee is that you only pay for the reviews that actually come down, so there's no financial risk in trying.

Do you offer refunds?

We work on a success/removal basis, so you only pay once we've confirmed the review is removed — that means we don't offer refunds. If the same reviewer posts another review afterward, we'll remove that one for half the price.

What information do you need to start?

Just a link to your Google Business Profile. We handle the audit and identify which reviews are worth pursuing before anything gets billed.
