How Often Should You Post on Google Business Profile?

GrowGigs Team

Posting frequency on Google Business Profile gets more attention than it probably deserves. Here's a realistic read on what it actually does and how often is actually worth it.

What posts on your profile actually are

Google Business Profile posts are short updates, offers, events, announcements, that appear on your listing and can show up in search and Maps results. They're closer to a lightweight social feed attached to your listing than a core ranking factor.

Does posting frequency affect your ranking

There's no publicly confirmed, reliable link between posting volume and local ranking position. Posts can indirectly support visibility, by giving searchers a reason to click through, by keeping a listing looking active and maintained, by surfacing a specific offer at the right moment, but treating posting cadence as a direct ranking lever tends to lead to disappointment when ranking doesn't move the way a "post daily" strategy promises.

What a reasonable cadence actually looks like

Posting something genuinely useful, a real offer, a real update, a real event, whenever you have one, beats a fixed daily schedule filled with filler content. For most small businesses, that's realistically a few times a month rather than daily. A profile with infrequent but substantive posts generally serves customers, and your own reputation, better than one with frequent, low-value posts that exist only to hit a quota.

Why low-value, high-frequency posting can backfire

Posts that are vague, repetitive, or clearly generated just to maintain a streak don't add anything for a customer browsing your listing, and a cluttered, repetitive post history can make a profile look more like it's being gamed than genuinely maintained. Quality and relevance matter more here than raw frequency. This is particularly worth keeping in mind if you're working with an agency or tool that bills by posting volume, more posts isn't automatically more value, and it's reasonable to ask what each post is actually meant to accomplish.

What's actually worth posting about

Real promotions or limited-time offers, genuine updates like new hours or a service change, events, and posts that answer a question customers commonly ask. If you don't have something genuinely worth posting, skipping that week is a better choice than posting filler just to keep a streak alive.

What matters more than frequency

If posting cadence isn't a reliable lever, where should the actual effort go? Accurate business information, a complete and correctly categorized profile, genuine reviews, and real photos of your actual business all have a far more established connection to how customers find and choose you. Posts are a supplement to those fundamentals, not a substitute for getting them right, and prioritizing them in the wrong order means spending time on the part that matters least while the parts that matter most sit unfinished.

A simple way to decide when to post

Ask whether a customer who saw this post would learn something they didn't already know and couldn't easily find elsewhere on your profile. A genuine seasonal offer, a real menu addition, a holiday closure, all pass that test. A generic "we're open for business" post with no new information generally doesn't. If you're not sure a post clears that bar, it's usually not worth publishing.

The honest takeaway

Posting on Google Business Profile is a minor, supplementary part of managing a listing, not a ranking hack. A realistic, substance-first cadence, posting when you actually have something worth sharing, serves your profile better than chasing a frequency target that isn't backed by a confirmed ranking benefit.
