FAQ

Common questions

If you don't see your question here, ask us directly, we reply fast.

Can you actually get a bad Google review removed?

Yes. Google removes reviews that violate its policies, including fake reviews, reviews from non-customers, spam, off-topic content, and conflict-of-interest posts. We identify which of your reviews qualify and file the removal requests on your behalf.

Do I pay if a review doesn't get removed?

No. We work on a pay-per-removal basis. You're only billed for the reviews and content we actually get taken down, never for attempts that don't succeed.

What if the negative review is real, can it still be removed?

Yes, in most cases. Review platforms allow removal for more reasons than people expect, and even a review that looks genuine on the surface often qualifies once we dig into the account history, the reviewer's actual connection to the business, or how it was posted. We review every flagged review case by case, this is exactly where our experience makes the difference.

Do you handle reviews outside of Google too?

Yes. Google is our specialty, but we also handle Trustpilot, Glassdoor, Yelp, and other major review platforms on request.

What's the difference between review removal and a takedown?

Review removal targets reviews on platforms like Google that violate that platform's own policies. A takedown is broader, it covers DMCA copyright claims, IP infringement, impersonation on social media, defamatory content, and cyberbullying, wherever it's hosted.

How long does the process take?

It depends on the platform and the volume of content involved. Straightforward Google review removals are often resolved within days, DMCA and legal takedowns can take longer depending on the host's response time.

How much does it cost to remove a Google review?

There's no flat fee since it depends on volume and platform, but there's no retainer or upfront cost either. You only pay for the reviews we actually get removed, so you'll know the exact cost before committing to anything.

Can I try to remove a review myself first?

Yes, and for a single obvious policy violation it's worth trying through Google's own reporting tool first. Where we add the most value is on reviews Google's automated system doesn't catch on the first pass, or when you're dealing with volume across multiple locations.

Do I need to work with someone local to me?

No. Review removal and content takedowns happen entirely online, so there's no advantage to hiring someone in your city. We work remotely with businesses across the USA, Canada, and worldwide, and location has never been a barrier to getting results.

Do you only work with the industries listed on your site?

No, that's a sample, not the full list. If your business has a Google profile and a bad review or harmful content is costing you customers, we can help, regardless of your industry.
