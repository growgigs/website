How to Remove a Fake Social Media Profile Impersonating Your Business

GrowGigs Team

A fake account using your business name, logo, or branding isn't just annoying, it can scam your customers and damage trust you've spent years building. Every major platform has a process for this, and they're all slightly different.

Document everything first

Screenshot the fake profile: its handle, bio, profile photo, and any posts. Fake accounts get taken down or edited quickly once they realize they've been spotted, so capture evidence before you report anything.

Facebook and Instagram (Meta)

Use the "Report" option on the profile itself and select impersonation as the reason. If you have a verified Page or Business account, Meta's Brand Rights Protection tools give verified businesses more direct reporting options. Trademark registration, if you have one, strengthens an impersonation claim but isn't strictly required to report a fake account impersonating your business.

X (formerly Twitter)

X has a specific impersonation report form separate from general abuse reports. You'll need to identify the account and explain how it's impersonating your business, logo, name, or branding used without authorization is the core of the claim.

LinkedIn

Report directly from the fake profile or company page. LinkedIn's process leans on business verification, having your own verified LinkedIn Page strengthens your report when claiming another page is impersonating your business.

TikTok

Report through the app's in-platform reporting tool, selecting impersonation as the category. TikTok's process typically requires you to confirm your identity as the business being impersonated.

What every platform wants to see

  • Proof you're the real business. A verified account, website, or business registration.
  • The specific impersonating elements. Logo, name, photos, or branding copied directly.
  • Evidence of harm or confusion, if applicable. Customers who reached out confused, or the account soliciting money or information.

If customers are being scammed

Warn your actual audience directly, a post on your real profile clarifying which account is legitimate helps limit damage while the report processes. This doesn't replace the removal report, but it protects customers in the meantime.

Timing

Clear-cut impersonation with a verified business behind the report often moves faster than other content disputes, platforms generally treat brand impersonation as a priority category. Still, expect anywhere from a couple of days to a couple of weeks depending on the platform and how backed up their review queue is.
