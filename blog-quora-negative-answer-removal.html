Can You Get a Negative Answer Removed From a Quora Thread About Your Business?

GrowGigs Team

Someone asks "Is [your business] worth it?" on Quora, and a critical answer ranks well enough in search that it shows up when a prospective customer looks you up. Unlike a review platform, Quora has no business-profile concept at all, every answer is just one person's response to a question, which changes both what counts as a violation and how you go about reporting one.

Why Quora isn't a review platform

A Google or Trustpilot review exists specifically to rate a business, with a policy framework built around that purpose. A Quora answer is a response to a question someone else asked, written by anyone with an opinion, including people who never did business with you at all. That means there's no "non-customer" policy to invoke the way there is on a review site, Quora doesn't require answerers to have any direct experience with what they're discussing.

What Quora will actually act on

  • Answers containing false factual claims presented as fact rather than opinion, this is the closest equivalent to a defamation report and usually needs the clearest evidence to support it.
  • Harassment or targeted abuse directed at your business or a named employee, rather than ordinary criticism.
  • Content violating Quora's own policies on spam, impersonation, or posting private information, reportable through the platform's standard flagging tool on each answer.

What Quora generally won't remove

An honestly negative opinion, even a harsh one, isn't a policy violation just because it hurts your business, and Quora's moderation leans toward protecting expressed opinion over removing unflattering but non-false content. This mirrors the same line drawn on the Reddit and LinkedIn platforms already covered: a bad opinion stays up, a false factual claim or policy violation is reportable.

How to actually file a report

Use the report option attached to the specific answer, not the question itself, and select the category that matches the actual problem (false information, harassment, policy violation) rather than a generic complaint. Quora's review process for individual answers tends to move slower than platforms built specifically around business content, since the moderation team is evaluating general content policy rather than a review-specific framework.

Why writing your own answer often matters more here

Because Quora has no review-response feature, the most effective tool you have, separate from any takedown request, is posting your own answer to the same question with your side of the story. A well-written, factual answer from the business itself frequently outranks or sits alongside a critical one in the thread, which does more for how a prospective customer reads the overall picture than a removal request working its way through moderation ever will on its own.

When the answer is actually search-visible defamation

If the answer makes a specific, false factual claim rather than an opinion, and it's ranking in search results tied to your business name, this overlaps with the broader search de-indexing option already covered, pursuing removal from the search results themselves as a parallel track to the Quora report, rather than waiting on one before trying the other.

What evidence actually strengthens a report

A screenshot of the answer with its timestamp, the exact question it's responding to, and, where possible, something showing the claim is false, a receipt, a policy document, correspondence that contradicts the stated facts, all give Quora's moderation team something concrete to evaluate rather than a dispute over opinion. A report that simply says "this is unfair" is far less likely to move than one that points to a specific, checkable factual error and explains plainly why it's wrong.

Why patience matters more here than on most platforms

Quora's content moderation isn't built around business reputation disputes the way a review platform's is, which means response times can run considerably longer and the outcome is less predictable even when a report is well-documented. Treating this as a multi-week process rather than something resolved in a few days helps set realistic expectations, and posting your own answer in the meantime means you're not left with nothing to show a prospective customer while the report works its way through.
